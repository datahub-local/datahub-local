Maintain the content domain: catalogue drift, links and assets, factual
freshness, roadmap and status, image staleness, and consistency.

Use the `site-content-maintainer` skill and follow it exactly. It owns the
checks, the edit rules, and the publish step. No cluster access is needed.

A no-op run publishes nothing and reports the domain as current.
